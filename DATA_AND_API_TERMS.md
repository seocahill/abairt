5. **Pass the content off as yours.** Where you show Abairt recordings or

6. **Train or sell a model on the content for commercial use.** Any use of

## What is welcome

- Forking the code, hosting your own copy and uploading your own recordings and
  content, free of charge, to build a community of your own.
- Listening, reading and searching on abairt.com, and in the official Abairt
  Android app.
- Free, non-commercial apps, courses, research, teaching and community projects
  that fetch from abairt.com in moderation and credit it, such as the Mayo
  Irish course.
- Non-commercial research and study of the material. Get in touch for bulk
  data.

If you want to build something that does not fit, or you are not sure whether it
does, ask first.
